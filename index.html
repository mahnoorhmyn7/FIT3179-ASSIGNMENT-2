Australian wildlife · Conservation evidence

Beyond the Exhibit

Follow the evidence from Australia’s threatened-species lists to zoo-sourced releases—and what researchers reported afterward.

01 · The conservation challenge

Where are threatened species recorded?

The dataset lists 700 threatened taxa across Australian states and territories. A taxon can appear on more than one state list. The map shows listed taxa, not the number of individual animals in the wild.

02 · From zoo to wild

Which species appear in translocation studies?

A 2026 review brings together 117 global study records of conservation translocations using zoo-sourced animals. Of these, 27 concern Australia. Thirteen taxa in the Australian records also appear in our threatened-species list.

03 · After release

What did the studies report?

Among the 16 Australian study records that match the threatened species list, authors coded 6 outcomes as success, 2 as failure and 8 as undetermined. These are reported study outcomes, not a measure of zoo wide success.

04 · About this evidence

Sources and limits

The threatened-species list is from data.gov.au. The translocation records are supplementary data for Blais et al. (2026), “Reach and Effectiveness of Conservation Translocations When Founder Animals Are Sourced From Zoos”.

The translocation file summarises published study records; it is not a complete list of current Australian zoo programs. Some outcomes are undetermined, and records may describe repeated studies of a program.
